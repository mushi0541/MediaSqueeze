=========================================
      MediaSqueeze Installation Guide    
=========================================

Welcome to MediaSqueeze! 

This application provides blazing-fast media compression utilizing your system's native hardware acceleration (GPU) capabilities.

-------------------------
 1. System Requirements  
-------------------------
- Operating System: Windows 10 / Windows 11 (64-bit)
- Processor: Modern multi-core CPU
- Graphics: (Optional but recommended) Nvidia, AMD, or Intel GPU for Hardware Acceleration.
- Memory: 4GB RAM minimum (8GB recommended for heavy 4K video compression)

-------------------------
 2. How to Install       
-------------------------
1. Double-click the installer executable (`MediaSqueeze_Setup.exe` or `.msi`).
2. Follow the on-screen prompts to choose your installation directory.
3. Once installed, launch "MediaSqueeze" from your Desktop or Start Menu.

-------------------------
 3. Basic Usage          
-------------------------
1. Drag and drop your video or image files directly into the center of the app.
2. Select your desired Quality, Preset, and Codec on the left sidebar.
   * Tip: If you have a dedicated graphics card, make sure to select "Nvidia (NVENC)", "AMD (AMF)", or "Intel (QSV)" under the Hardware Acceleration dropdown for massive speed boosts!
3. Click "Compress Now".
4. Once completed, click the small "Folder" icon next to the completed file to instantly locate your optimized media!
